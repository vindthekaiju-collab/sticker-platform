where the images come from, and how to reach us.

where the images come from

Every sticker on stickky is built from an image that is either in the public domain (museum open-access collections, government photography, works whose copyright has expired) or published under a Creative Commons Attribution licence that allows commercial use with credit. Captions and crops are ours.

Every pack ships with a CREDITS.txt listing the source page, the author and the licence of each image. Attribution for CC BY sources is also shown on the pack's store page.

real people

Some packs use historical or official photographs of real, public figures. These images are free to use under their licences, and we present them in the spirit of the memes they already are. We do not imply endorsement by anyone pictured.

think something should come down?

If you believe a sticker infringes your copyright, your right of publicity, a trademark, or your privacy, tell us. Send the pack name, the sticker, and what you hold rights to. We remove first and discuss after: a verified request is taken down within 48 hours, and the pack is re-issued without that image.

hello@stickky.xyz

The same address works for licensing questions, corrections to a credit line, or if you are the author of a CC BY image and want your attribution changed.
